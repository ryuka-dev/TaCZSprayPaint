## 1.0.0 (2026-11-26)

First release. Works with Timeless and Classics Zero 1.1.8-hotfix, 1.1.8-hotfix2 and 1.2.0-hotfix on Minecraft
1.20.1 (Forge 47).

- Spray Can: gives a gun a random finish (pattern, grade and wear), shown on a reveal screen with the painted gun.
- Spray Can Case: gives ten finishes at once; the player picks one. Unpicked finishes stay on the gun and can be
  picked later with `/taczspray choose`.
- Paint Remover: removes a gun's finish.
- 19 patterns, five grades and five wear levels, on the 37 guns of TaCZ's default gun pack.
- Configuration in `config/taczspray/`: custom finishes, series with their own odds, crafting switches, grade and
  wear odds, and `/taczspray give` for cans and cases of a series or of one finish.
- Under shader packs that read LabPBR maps (with Oculus), painted guns keep their specular maps, and metal
  finishes look like metal.
- 20 languages.
