The files in this folder (the .bin region files, regions.json and the finish.json files that point at them) are made
from the models of the default gun pack of Timeless and Classics Zero (TaCZ), by Serene Wave Studio and Timeless Squad,
art by NekoCrane, Receke and Pos_2333: https://github.com/MCModderAnchor/TACZ

For each gun that can be painted they keep the corners and texture coordinates of the faces that get painted, and
which texture pixels to paint, converted to TaCZ Spray Paint's own format. They hold none of the pack's texture pixels.
